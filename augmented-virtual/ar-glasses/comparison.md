### Sources/verification

XREAL's current lineup confirms One Pro at $599, One at $399, 1S at $449, Air 2 Pro at $249 and Air 2 at $199; XREAL Eye is an optional $99 accessory. ([XREAL US Shop][1])

VITURE's current Beast is 88g, 58°, 1920×1200 and 1,250-nit perceived brightness; Luma Ultra is 83g, 52° and 1920×1200. VITURE currently lists Beast at $549 and Luma Ultra at $599. ([Viture][2])

VITURE Pro 2 is a particularly interesting budget/lightweight model: **63g**, 1920×1080, 50°, 120Hz and 1,600 nits, currently $299. ([Viture][3])

RayNeo Air 4 Pro is 76g, 46°, 1920×1080, 120Hz and 1,200 nits with HDR10, currently $299; RayNeo also has the newer 78g GT Max at roughly $429. ([RayNeo][4])

URXR One claims 93g, dual 2448×2064 micro-OLEDs, 90° FOV, 6DoF, hand tracking and video see-through without a puck or base stations; its official site currently shows $799 Kickstarter pricing versus $999 MSRP. ([Unseen Reality][5])

XREAL Aura is a different class: 70° FOV, 1920×1200/eye, 6DoF, hand tracking, cameras and an Android XR/Snapdragon Reality Elite puck with 12/256GB or 16/512GB configurations. XREAL says final retail will be **no more than $1,500**. ([XREAL][6])

Bigscreen Beyond 2/2e are 107–108g PCVR headsets starting around $959, with 6DoF SteamVR tracking and 2560×2560 micro-OLEDs. 

Shiftall's MeganeX 8K Mark II is 179g, 3552×3840/eye, 90Hz, 10-bit HDR, 6DoF SteamVR and $1,899. ([Shiftall][7])

VIVE Flow is 189g and currently $199.99 with controller, but it remains much more limited than the newer spatial-computing glasses. ([VIVE][8])
