## 1. Gmail Smart Features: ON vs. OFF

Setting: Gmail → Settings → General → Smart features → “Turn on smart features in Gmail, Chat and Meet.”

| Privacy / functionality            | ON                                                                                                                                    | OFF                                                                                                                                                                    |
| ---------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Use of email content               | Gmail, Chat and Meet may use your content and activity to personalize features in those products.                                     | This use of content and activity for the listed smart features is disabled.                                                                                            |
| Smart Compose                      | Suggested text while writing emails.                                                                                                  | Unavailable through this setting.                                                                                                                                      |
| Smart Reply                        | Suggested quick replies to messages.                                                                                                  | Unavailable through this setting in Gmail.                                                                                                                             |
| Inbox categorization               | Automatic categories such as Primary, Social and Promotions.                                                                          | These automatic categorization features are unavailable.                                                                                                               |
| Summary cards                      | Information such as package tracking and travel details can appear above relevant emails.                                             | These summary cards are unavailable through this setting.                                                                                                              |
| Important email nudges             | Certain reminders and high-priority notifications may be available.                                                                   | Related smart features controlled by this setting are disabled.                                                                                                        |
| AI and personalization             | Enables the listed personalized smart features. Data may also be used to improve these features, according to Google's documentation. | Disables the listed smart-feature experiences. It does not establish a blanket prohibition on all data processing or AI-related uses.                                  |
| Email delivery and storage         | Normal Gmail functionality.                                                                                                           | Normal Gmail functionality continues.                                                                                                                                  |
| Spam, malware and account security | Gmail continues its security protections.                                                                                             | These protections are not disabled by this setting.                                                                                                                    |
| Advertising                        | Google says Gmail message content is not used to select ads.                                                                          | Turning this setting off is not necessary to disable Gmail-content-based ad targeting under Google's stated policy; advertising personalization has separate controls. |

## 2. Important distinction: OFF does not mean zero processing

Google's documentation makes clear that turning off Smart Features removes the listed personalized experiences, but does not stop Gmail from processing messages to operate its service.

Also, this switch is separate from the two Google Workspace controls:

| Separate setting                        | What ON permits                                                                                                                   | What OFF disables                                                                   |
| --------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Smart features in Google Workspace      | Cross-product Workspace experiences, such as showing Gmail events in Calendar, personalized search and supported Gemini features. | Those smart features across Workspace products.                                     |
| Smart features in other Google products | Supported experiences using Workspace data in other products, including certain Gemini, Search, Maps and Wallet features.         | Those cross-product smart features, subject to other controls and explicit sharing. |

## My assessment

If your priority is privacy rather than convenience, turning the Gmail-specific Smart Features setting OFF is a reasonable choice. You give up automatic categorization, suggested replies and certain summaries, but retain ordinary email functionality.

I'd also review the two separate Workspace settings rather than assuming the Gmail switch controls them.

The important qualification is that Google's January 2025 announcement explicitly said the revised controls did not change its underlying data-handling practices. They give users more granular control over smart-feature experiences; they are not a blanket opt-out from every form of data processing.
