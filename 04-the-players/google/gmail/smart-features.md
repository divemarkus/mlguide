# Gmail Smart Features: ON vs. OFF

I reviewed Google's January 7, 2025 announcement and its current Gmail Help documentation. The key finding is that Smart Features is not one universal switch. Google now separates controls for features within Gmail, across Google Workspace, and in other Google products. Each setting controls a different use of your data.&#x20;

## 1. Gmail, Chat and Meet — the setting you're asking about

| Category          | ON                                                                                                                 | OFF                                                                                                |
| ----------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Data use          | Google can use your content and activity in Gmail, Chat and Meet to personalize your experience in those products. | This use of your content and activity for the listed smart features is disabled.                   |
| Smart Compose     | Suggested text while writing emails.                                                                               | Unavailable through this setting.                                                                  |
| Smart Reply       | Suggested quick replies to messages.                                                                               | Unavailable through this setting.                                                                  |
| Inbox categories  | Automatic categorization, such as Primary, Social and Promotions.                                                  | These smart categorization features are unavailable; you may need to manage your inbox manually.   |
| Summary cards     | Information cards for things such as packages, travel and events.                                                  | These summary cards are unavailable through this setting.                                          |
| Email delivery    | Normal Gmail operation.                                                                                            | Normal Gmail operation continues.                                                                  |
| Spam and security | Gmail continues its security and abuse protections.                                                                | Gmail continues its security and abuse protections; turning this switch off does not disable them. |

## 3. What does this mean for privacy?

| Privacy question                                                           | What the documentation establishes                                                                                                     |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Does ON permit use of email content for smart features?                    | Yes. Google explicitly says the relevant settings allow content and activity to be used for the specified personalization experiences. |
| Does OFF mean Google stops processing email?                               | No. Gmail still processes messages to operate the service and provide security protections.                                            |
| Does OFF mean Google can never access my messages?                         | No. It is not a blanket prohibition on all processing or access.                                                                       |
| Does OFF mean my email is no longer used for advertising?                  | Google's stated policy is that Gmail message content is not used to select ads. Advertising controls are separate.                     |
| Does OFF disable every AI feature?                                         | No. Separately controlled features, explicit sharing, and other product settings may still apply.                                      |
| Does ON mean Google is training a general-purpose AI model on every email? | The setting alone does not establish that. It authorizes specified smart-feature uses, not a blanket conclusion about model training.  |

## 4. My assessment

For someone who values privacy and wants to minimize unnecessary use of personal data, I'd distinguish the three settings this way:

| Setting               | Privacy-conscious choice | Main sacrifice                                                              |
| --------------------- | ------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Gmail, Chat and Meet  | OFF                      | Smart Compose, Smart Reply, automatic categories and summary cards          |
| Google Workspace      | OFF if not needed        | Workspace integrations, contextual search and supported Gemini conveniences |
| Other Google products | OFF                      | Cross-product personalization using Workspace content and activity          |

This is a choice about reducing data use for personalization, not a claim that Google stops processing or storing email when the controls are disabled.

### One important clarification about Google's announcement

Google's January 2025 article describes the Workspace controls as providing more granular choice. It also explicitly says the change did not alter Google's underlying data-handling practices. In other words, the new controls let you choose which smart-feature experiences to use; they are not a new promise that Google will no longer process your data.&#x20;

My recommendation: If you don't use Gmail's AI conveniences, turn off its Smart Features setting. Then review the two separate Workspace settings rather than assuming the Gmail switch covers everything. That is the most direct way to reduce the smart-feature uses of your email data while retaining ordinary Gmail functionality.
