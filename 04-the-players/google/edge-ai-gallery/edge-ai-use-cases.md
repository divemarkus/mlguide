# 1. Travel Translation (Excellent Use Case)

## Scenario

* No roaming
* No cellular data
* Rural area
* Subway/tunnel
* Airplane mode
* International travel

## What Local AI Can Do

* translate signs
* translate menus
* live conversation assistance
* OCR foreign text from camera
* summarize directions

Example:

```text id="lg2iv4"
Point camera at Japanese train station sign →
Gemma translates locally →
No internet needed
```

This is one of the strongest edge-AI scenarios because:

* low latency
* privacy
* works anywhere
